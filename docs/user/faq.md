# Questions new makers ask

Short answers to the questions most makers have before they start. Each answer describes OpenPlush as it is today; a question whose answer depends on a part of the tool that is not ready yet is not here.

## Is it free?

Yes. OpenPlush costs nothing, has no paid version and no subscription, and the patterns you make with it are yours to use, share or sell. It is open source: anyone can read how it works, and it is released under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/openplush/openplush/blob/main/LICENSE-MIT) or the [Apache 2.0 licence](https://github.com/openplush/openplush/blob/main/LICENSE-APACHE), whichever you prefer.

## Does my model leave my computer?

No. OpenPlush runs entirely on your own device. Your model, your seams and your pattern are never uploaded anywhere, there is no account to create, and nothing about what you make or how you use the tool is collected or sent to anyone. It works without an internet connection.

## Which files can I open?

Models saved as OBJ, STL or PLY files, which is what most 3D modelling and sculpting programs can export. The model is read as it was saved: OpenPlush never changes the file on your disk. A model saved in another format does not open; save it as one of those three from your modelling program first.
